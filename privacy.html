Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or ads. I only get personal information about you if you choose to send it to me, and I only use it to reply and, if we work together, to run that work. I never sell it.

Who I am

abc marketing is a trading name of Abbie Costain, a sole trader based in Bath, UK. I'm responsible for the personal information collected through this website (the "controller" under UK data protection law).

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email me at amscostain@gmail.com.

What I collect and why

When you get in touch. If you contact me through the form on this site or by email, I'll receive the details you give me: usually your name, job title, company, email address, phone number and your message. I use these to reply and to talk about whether we could work together.

My lawful basis is legitimate interests (responding to people who contact me about my services). If your enquiry leads to work, I also rely on taking steps to enter into a contract with you, and then on the contract itself.

When you visit the site. I don't collect anything about your visit myself. Two services that make the site work do receive basic technical information, like your IP address and browser details:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running.
  • Google Fonts provides the font this site uses. Your browser fetches it from Google, which means Google receives your IP address. Google says it doesn't use this to build profiles or for advertising.

I don't see or control these logs. You can read GitHub's privacy statement and Google Fonts' privacy FAQ for more.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies or use similar technology to track you, so there's nothing to accept or decline.

Who I share your information with

I don't share your details with anyone for their own marketing. The only people who handle it are the service providers I use to run my business, such as my email provider and the hosting and font services above. They process it on my behalf or under their own privacy terms.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it

If your enquiry doesn't lead to work, I'll delete your details within 12 months of our last contact. If we do work together, I'll keep your details for as long as the work lasts, then for as long as I need to for tax and accounting records, usually up to six years.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how I use it
  • send your information to you or someone else in a usable format

You can also object to me using your information based on legitimate interests. To do any of these, email me at amscostain@gmail.com. It's free, and I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal information, for example by adding a new service, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
