About me

Hi, I'm Abbie

... The she behind abc!

I've spent nearly a decade in marketing, starting out in PR, social media and communications before finding my feet in B2B tech and SaaS, specialising in demand generation, lifecycle and product marketing.

I've worked across early-stage start-ups, growing scale-ups and larger enterprises in the UK, US and Europe, building a pretty varied marketing toolkit along the way.
