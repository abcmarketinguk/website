Marketing made a little simpler

abc marketing is me, Abbie Costain, working with a small number of teams at a time. The name started with my initials, but I like that it also says what I'm aiming for: marketing that feels as easy as abc.

I work as part of your team rather than an agency on the outside, so you get experienced support without the overheads or the long contracts.

About me

Hi, I'm Abbie

I've worked in marketing for over eight years, most recently in B2B SaaS at Plentific and Happeo, where I looked after demand generation and product marketing. I started abc marketing to help smaller teams get that same experience, without needing to hire someone full time.

I'm based in Bath and work with teams all over the UK. I like to fit in quietly, do good work and leave things in better shape than I found them.
