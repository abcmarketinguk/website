About abc marketing

The background

The way businesses work is changing fast, especially with AI, and priorities can shift from one quarter to the next. I set up abc marketing to bring my years of in-house experience to B2B businesses that need good marketing, and need it to get going quickly.

Whether you're building your marketing function from scratch, covering time off, or keeping things moving while you find the right full-time hire, I can step in for as long or as short a time as you need, from a single project or launch to something longer term. Either way, I start by listening and getting to know your goals, your customers and what really makes your business tick, so the strategy we land on is one we can act on. I slot in like one of your own team, see it through and move with you as your business grows.

I'm a one-person team, so if we work together, you'll be working with me directly. I support with clients directly and through partner marketing agencies who want an experienced marketer to help deliver work for their clients.
